# Term Project Requirements

A summary of the 475/575 term project assignment: build a database-driven web application. Groups have 2 or 3 students, and the captain submits each stage. Brightspace has the original assignment and rubric.

## Stages

| Stage | Deliverable | Weight | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `Stage1_<ID>.doc` | — | Team name, members with UA email, captain. |
| 2 | `Stage2_<ID>.doc` | 15% | Title, summary, domain, usefulness (and how it differs from similar sites), realness of data, planned basic and advanced functions. |
| 3 | `Stage3_<ID>.doc` | 10% | Final database and platform, relational schema with all keys and constraints, real data sources, task division, timeline with milestones. |
| 4 | Demo (5–8 min) + `Stage4_<ID>.doc` | 15% | Demo: real data, insert/update/delete, at least one search, plan for advanced functions. Report: title, summary, databases and platforms, how real data was obtained, detailed functions developed, planned advanced functions. |
| 5 | `Stage5_<ID>.doc` | 15% | Title, current status (functions and real data), implementation of the advanced functions. |
| 6 | Demo (10–15 min) | 25% | Basic functions plus at least two advanced functions (three for 575). |
| 7 | `Stage7_<ID>.zip` | 15% | Final report (implementation details, screenshots, contributions, lessons learned, future work), source code, setup instructions, slides, list of changes since the demo. |
| — | Individual grade | 5% | |

## Functions

- **Basic:** insert, update, and delete records; search queries, at least one of which joins multiple tables.
- **Advanced:** at least two (three for 575). Each must be useful to users, much more technically challenging than the basic functions, and creative or novel compared with similar applications. At the demo, the team must explain the problem and the solution in detail.

## Real Data

The system must contain real data. If users enter the data, the site must be fully working and publicly reachable at least one week before the final presentation, and the instructor must be emailed when it goes live.

## Grading Notes

Grading considers how well the project meets its proposal, how well the system works, its usability, and the readability of the code. Presentations are graded on preparation, staying within the time limit, clarity, and answers to questions.
